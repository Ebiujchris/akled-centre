Who we are

Independent expertise.
Practical outcomes.

Allied Knowledge, Learning and Development Centre is a Uganda-based research and consulting institute established in 2021.

We support government, civil society, communities, and the private sector to design projects, generate evidence, strengthen institutions, and improve performance.
