About AKLED

Knowledge that moves us forward.

Allied Knowledge, Learning and Development Centre — practical research and consulting for stronger institutions and communities.

Background

Rooted in evidence. Focused on action.

Allied Knowledge, Learning and Development Centre (AKLED Centre) is a Uganda-based research institute, established and registered in 2021. Its early work focused on bringing research into policy and decision-making. Since 2026, AKLED’s aim has broadened: to build knowledge and enhance learning that facilitate insightful action and improve business and institutional performance.

AKLED designs and builds projects, including physical infrastructure; prepares concepts and proposals; collects and analyses data; and develops customized strategic and business plans and other documents. A broad network of consultants provides technical, strategic, and advisory services to agencies, communities, groups, and individuals.

Vision

A model centre for consulting solutions, capacity building, action research, monitoring, evaluation and learning which contributes to socioeconomic transformation.

Our uniqueness

Insight shaped around your context

We work to understand the organization, business, community, or sector before recommending a way forward.

Evidence into decisions

We support civil society, the private sector, and government to plan, implement, monitor, and evaluate cultural, social, health, and development programmes and projects. Our research generates ideas that inform understanding, policy, practice, and public debate.

Rigorous, contextual work

We gather and interpret relevant facts to help clients make informed decisions for their businesses, communities, and organizations. We document findings and recommendations and share them with relevant stakeholders to support meaningful use.

Experience across disciplines

Our consultant network brings experience in research, monitoring and evaluation, business development, financial services, agribusiness, and financial inclusion.

Collaborative delivery

We work with ministries and government agencies, civil society, private entities, communities, cooperative societies, and other partners to shape useful, tailored solutions.
